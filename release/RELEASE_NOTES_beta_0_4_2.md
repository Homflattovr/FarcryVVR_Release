# Far Cry 5 VR Beta 0.4.2

Replaces Beta 0.4.1 and keeps its crash fix. Everything else is as in [Beta 0.4](RELEASE_NOTES_beta_0_4.md).

## New

- **Button prompts that match your controllers.** The game's prompts (interact, reload, jump, the vehicle's throttle and
  brake, the weapon wheel, the map and pause) now show the VR controller's buttons, with the buttons the mod actually
  maps them to (for example interact and reload on Y, jump on A, a car's throttle on A and brake on X). Keyboard
  prompts, which the game shows because the mod aims with mouse moves, are drawn as the matching controller button too.
  Choose the style in F11 > HUD and menus > Button icons: Auto (from your controllers), Touch (Quest), Index, or Off for
  the game's own icons. Not yet checked in a headset.

## Changed

- F11 > Gestures: the holster and the shoulder weapon slots are no longer marked "experimental".

## Install

Same as Beta 0.4: extract `FC5VR_beta_0_4_2.zip` into the game folder and replace every file.
