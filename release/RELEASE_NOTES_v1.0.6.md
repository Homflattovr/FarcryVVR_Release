# Far Cry 5 VR 1.0.6

An update to 1.0.5. It replaces 1.0.5 and keeps everything in it. Download `FC5VR_v1.0.6.zip` below and follow the
[install steps](https://github.com/Homflattovr/FarcryVVR_Release#install).

> **Strongly recommended: Virtual Desktop with its OpenXR runtime (VDXR).** SteamVR works but is not recommended.
>
> **If you have a problem with the hands or legs, change your skin (outfit) first.**

## Fixes

- **Driving and shooting: the shots follow the gun.** With the sidearm out while driving, the bullets went straight
  ahead of the vehicle whatever the gun pointed at. The game's aim now follows the gun in your hand, as on foot, and
  goes back to the vehicle's front when you put the gun away. F11 switch: "Vehicles: driving with a gun out, the
  shots follow the gun".
- **Driving and shooting: both arms show.** The hand holding the gun now has its forearm and upper arm, and the hand
  on the wheel or handlebars keeps its arm too (only the hands showed before). F11 switches: "Vehicles: driving with
  a gun out, the gun hand has its arm" and "... the other hand keeps its arm".
- **No more accidental punches.** With no weapon in the hand (or the weapon on the sling), an open hand that moved
  into someone could throw the game's melee. A punch now needs a closed fist: hold the grip. F11 > Gestures > Melee:
  "Punch only with a closed fist (grip held)".

## Changes

- **Gesture driving: pull your hand away to let go.** A hand pulled more than 25 cm off the wheel, handlebars or
  stick lets go of it even with the grip still held, so it follows your controller again instead of staying on the
  wheel (and no longer steers from far away). Grip again on the wheel to take it back. F11 gesture driving: "Pull
  away to let go" (0: only releasing the grip lets go, as before).
