Far Cry 5 VR - 1.0.3: known issues

Things we already know about (no need to report them, unless they look worse than described):

- Made and tested on a Quest 3 with Virtual Desktop (VDXR), an RTX 4090 and the Steam copy of the game. Virtual Desktop with VDXR is strongly recommended. Other headsets and OpenXR runtimes, and the Ubisoft Connect or Epic copies, should work but are untested. SteamVR works but is not recommended.
- Hands or legs missing or wrong: change your skin (outfit) first. Some outfits are drawn in ways the mod does not read yet.
- Performance on an RTX 4090: about 50 fps in Stereo, 80+ in AFW. If it is too slow, use AFW (F8), a lower DLSS mode, or OFXR Bridge for frame generation.
- Cutscenes play in 3D around you. If one feels uncomfortable, F11 > Main > Cutscenes can show them on a flat screen instead.
- The full body (F11 > Main > You see) is newer than the hands-only view: arms or legs can sometimes take an odd pose, mostly in vehicles and scripted scenes. It is on by default: F11 > Main > You see > Hands only turns it off.
- Melee with the weapon in your hand is experimental (its hits are on by default).
- Small culling flashes can still happen at the edges of the view.
- Water: short black flickers on the water while turning, and far rivers that end in a straight line (that last one is the game's own).
- With frame generation, menus can blink black.
- AFW is experimental, with visible artifacts: Stereo is the recommended mode.

When something looks wrong, please send: what you did, Stereo or AFW, a short video if you can, and the file fc5vr_log.txt from the game's bin folder (right after closing the game).

If the game does not start in VR (it closes, stays black or starts flat): start it with FC5VR_Launcher.exe. When the game closes during the start or crashes, the launcher offers to save FC5VR_report_<date>.zip in the bin folder; send that zip (its "Make a report" button makes one at any time). It holds the mod's log and crash report, your VR runtime's log, your system details and Windows' error events for the game. Nothing is uploaded.
