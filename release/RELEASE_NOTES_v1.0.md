# Far Cry 5 VR 1.0

The first full release. Download `FC5VR_v1.0.zip` below and follow the
[install steps](https://github.com/Homflattovr/FarcryVVR_Release#install).

> **Strongly recommended: Virtual Desktop with its OpenXR runtime (VDXR)**, plus OFXR Bridge and a high-bitrate
> codec. SteamVR works but is not recommended.
>
> **If you have a problem with the hands or legs, change your skin (outfit) first.**

## Some of the biggest features

- **True stereo VR through OpenXR**, with DLSS per eye (or FSR on other cards) and culling that follows your eyes.
- **Your weapon in your hand**: shots go where the barrel and sights point, two-handed aiming, recoil, scopes that
  magnify, gunstock calibration.
- **Manual reloading for every kind of gun**: magazines, pumps, levers, bolts, revolvers, belts and launchers, with
  your ammo on your left hip.
- **Full body (optional)**: arms and legs drawn under you, calibrated to your size, seated in vehicles, with your gear
  worn on the body.
- **A bow you draw with your own hands**, plus a sling, shoulder slots, a holster and throwables on your chest.
- **Your hands on the real steering wheel, handlebars or stick**, shooting your sidearm while driving, and usable
  vehicle turrets and mounted guns.
- **Cutscenes in 3D around you**, with their button prompts and a recenter when they end.
- **A floating HUD, ammo on your wrist and markers on their targets**, and VR controller button prompts in the game.
- **An F11 menu with a Main tab** for the common choices, and a launcher that checks your install and game options.

## New since Beta 0.4.6

- Full body and arms option (F11 > Main), with a T-pose calibration popup and the body seated in vehicles.
- Hands on the vehicle's own wheel, handlebars or stick; vehicle turrets work like mounted guns; mortar aiming;
  bomber view and its HUD; the right stick turns you as a passenger.
- Cutscenes in 3D by default, button prompts shown in cutscenes and scripted scenes, the view recentred when a
  cutscene ends, smoother riding cutscene cameras, no cyan streak following the controller.
- A weapon tips popup the first time you pick up a gun, and a loading popup capped at 4 seconds.
- Melee with the weapon in your hand (experimental), bow aiming fixes, magazine position sliders, smoother walking
  over bumps and bodies.
- SteamVR: the upside-down picture is detected and corrected.
- Fixes for the black squares in the lighting, a doubled objective marker, short stutters and a crash when starting
  a new game.
- A cleaned-up F11 menu with a new Main tab; the launcher gains an install checks panel and a button that sets the
  game options for you.
