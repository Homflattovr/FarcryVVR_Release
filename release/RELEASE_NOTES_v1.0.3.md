# Far Cry 5 VR 1.0.3: hotfix

A hotfix for 1.0.2. It replaces 1.0.2 and keeps everything in it. Download `FC5VR_v1.0.3.zip` below and follow the
[install steps](https://github.com/Homflattovr/FarcryVVR_Release#install).

> **Strongly recommended: Virtual Desktop with its OpenXR runtime (VDXR).** SteamVR works but is not recommended.
>
> **If you have a problem with the hands or legs, change your skin (outfit) first.**

## Fixes

- **Scopes work again after you change the optic.** A scope such as the ACOG or the SPECTER was taken for a red dot
  (the game zooms them only a little), and a gun could keep the 1x data of the optic it had before. Now a magnifying
  scope always gets the scope picture at the game's own zoom, and data learnt with another kind of optic is set aside
  (not deleted) and learnt again: aim down sights once with the new optic.
- **The spare magazine stays at your left hip.** With the full body on, the magazine follows the drawn body's belt;
  if that place is ever wrong (inside the body, or up at the throwables on the chest), the magazine now goes back to
  its usual hip place below your head, where you reach for it.
